P0 — Critical Fixes Must fix before launch

!

7 Pages in Sitemap Don't Exist → Will 404

The sitemap.xml references 7 URLs with no corresponding HTML files: /about, /contact, /financing, /coupons, /reviews, /blog, /privacy-policy. Google will crawl these, get 404s, and penalize crawl efficiency. The llms.txt also references the non-existent /contact page.

Impact: All pages · Fix: Build all 7 pages or remove from sitemap · Owner: Dev · Est: 8–12 hrs
!

Zero og:image and twitter:image Tags — Site-Wide

Not a single page has og:image or twitter:image meta tags. Any time someone shares a page on social media, messaging apps, or AI-generated link previews, there's no image preview. This kills social sharing click-through rates and looks unprofessional.

Impact: All 28 pages · Fix: Add branded OG image (1200×630) to every page · Owner: Design + Dev · Est: 3 hrs
!

Zero BreadcrumbList Schema — Site-Wide

No page has BreadcrumbList structured data. Google uses this for rich snippets and site hierarchy understanding. Every service page should show: Home → Services → AC Repair. Every area page: Home → Service Areas → Nashville. This is a direct ranking signal for navigation structure.

Impact: All 28 pages · Fix: Add BreadcrumbList JSON-LD to every page · Owner: Dev · Est: 2 hrs
!

Zero AggregateRating Schema — Site-Wide

No page has AggregateRating structured data. Competitors showing star ratings in SERPs get significantly higher click-through rates. This is worth 10 points in the scoring rubric and is the single biggest SERP visibility gap. Need review data from Google Business Profile to populate this.

Impact: All pages, especially homepage + service pages · Fix: Add AggregateRating to LocalBusiness schema · Owner: Dev + Client (review data) · Est: 2 hrs
!

Homepage Title and H1 Missing "Best" Keyword

The homepage title is Nashville HVAC Experts | AC Repair & Heating | Hughes HVAC Services and the H1 is Nashville's Honest HVAC Team. Neither contains "Best" — the primary keyword strategy for the entire site. The homepage is the highest-authority page and needs "Best HVAC Company Nashville" in both title and H1.

Impact: Homepage rankings · Fix: Update title to "Best HVAC Company Nashville TN | AC & Heating | Hughes HVAC" and H1 to include "Best" · Owner: Dev · Est: 15 min
!

All 17 Area Page Titles Missing "Best" Keyword

Every area page title follows HVAC Service in [City], TN | Hughes HVAC — none use "Best." The meta descriptions do, but titles carry far more weight. Should be Best HVAC Service in [City], TN | Hughes HVAC.

Impact: All 17 area pages · Fix: Prepend "Best" to all area page titles · Owner: Dev · Est: 30 min
!

All 17 Area Page H1s Missing "Best" Keyword

Every area page H1 is HVAC Service in [City], TN — no "Best." H1 is the strongest on-page signal. Combined with the title gap, these pages have zero "Best" keyword presence in their two most important elements.

Impact: All 17 area pages · Fix: Change all H1s to "Best HVAC Service in [City], TN" · Owner: Dev · Est: 30 min
!

All 10 Service Page H1s Missing "Best" Keyword

Service page titles were updated with "Best" (7 of 10), but every single H1 still uses the old format: AC Repair in Nashville & Middle Tennessee. The title says "Best" but the H1 doesn't match — this is a title/H1 alignment problem that weakens keyword signals.

Impact: All 10 service pages · Fix: Match H1s to "Best" title pattern · Owner: Dev · Est: 30 min
!

3 Service Page Titles Still Missing "Best"

furnace-repair, heat-pump-service, and emergency-hvac still have non-"Best" titles. furnace-repair: Furnace & Heater Repair Nashville TN | Same-Day Service | Hughes HVAC. These need the "Best" prefix for keyword alignment.

Impact: 3 service pages · Fix: Update titles to "Best Furnace Repair...", "Best Heat Pump...", "Best Emergency HVAC..." · Owner: Dev · Est: 15 min
!

Toxic Backlink Profile — 95%+ PBN Spam (Semrush Data)

Semrush backlink analysis reveals Hughes HVAC's existing backlink profile is almost entirely PBN (private blog network) spam originating from Singapore and Moldova. This is a Google penalty risk, not an authority builder. Before investing in new link building, the existing toxic links need to be identified and disavowed through Google Search Console. A clean backlink profile is the baseline for any off-site SEO campaign.

Impact: Entire domain authority · Fix: Audit with Semrush Backlink Audit tool → build disavow file → submit to Google Search Console · Owner: SEO · Est: 4 hrs
!

Zero Real Images on Entire Site — No Image SEO

Every page uses only inline SVG icons. There are no photographs, no hero images, no team photos, no equipment images. This means: zero image search visibility (Google Image Search drives significant HVAC traffic), no image alt text signals, no og:image source, and no visual trust signals. Competitors all have real photography on every page.

Impact: All 28 pages · Fix: Add hero images, team photos, equipment images with keyword-rich alt text · Owner: Design + Client (photos) · Est: 6–10 hrs

Top 3 Ranking Playbook What guarantees rankings

1

Week 1: Fix All P0 On-Page Issues

Update every title and H1 to include "Best." Add BreadcrumbList and AggregateRating schema to all pages. Add og:image and twitter:image tags. This is the highest-ROI work — costs nothing but development time and immediately strengthens every page's ranking signals.

2

Week 1–2: Build Missing Pages

Build all 7 missing pages (About, Contact, Financing, Coupons, Reviews, Blog shell, Privacy Policy). This eliminates sitemap 404s, completes the site architecture, and adds critical E-E-A-T and conversion pages that competitors all have.

3

Week 2: Add Real Photography

Get professional photos of: the Hughes HVAC truck/van, the team, Chris working on a unit, before/after installs, equipment. Add 2–3 images per page with keyword-rich alt text like "Best AC repair technician Nashville TN." This unlocks Google Image Search traffic and dramatically improves og:image sharing.

4

Week 2–3: Install GA4 + Search Console + Schema Validation

Deploy GA4 with event tracking for phone clicks and form submissions. Verify in Google Search Console. Submit sitemap. Validate all schema with Google's Rich Results Test. Set up conversion tracking to measure everything from day one.

5

Week 3–4: Build Citations + Directory Listings

Submit to top 50 local directories: Google Business Profile, Yelp, BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, NextDoor, Yellow Pages, Manta, CitySearch, and Nashville-specific directories. Ensure NAP consistency across all. This is the single biggest factor for local pack rankings.

6

Month 2: Launch Content Hub

Publish 2 blog posts per week targeting informational keywords: "how to tell if AC needs freon Nashville", "best thermostat for Nashville homes", "furnace maintenance checklist Tennessee", etc. Each post should internally link to 2–3 service pages and 1–2 area pages. Target 20+ posts in the first 60 days.

7

Month 2–3: Review Generation Campaign

Systematically request Google reviews from every completed job. Target 50+ reviews in 90 days. Reviews are the #1 local pack ranking factor and feed the AggregateRating schema. Set up automated post-service review request emails or SMS through JustCall.

8

Month 3+: Link Building + PR

Pursue local backlinks: Nashville Business Journal, TN Home Builders Association, local charity sponsorships, "best HVAC Nashville" roundup articles. Target 15–20 referring domains in the first 6 months. Off-site authority is the pillar that scored 30/100 — it's the long game but the decisive one.
